About the project

Designed during my semester abroad in Portugal, this poster was created for the Porto/Post/Doc Festival. The distorted letterforms echo the festival’s focus on pushing boundaries, questioning conventions, and reframing reality through film.

The bold color accents add vibrancy and energy, reflecting Porto’s creative pulse and the festival’s role as a meeting point for innovation in film and media.
