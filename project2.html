About the project

Großstadtzoo is a design studio founded in 2016 and based in Berlin, with the noble goal of making the world a little bit better. At its core is the animated ß in "Großstadtzoo", introducing a dynamic and vibrant identity element that brings movement and energy to the brand.

The modular grid structure allows for flexible content presentation, while the bold red accent color reinforces the studio’s confident and socially engaged character.
