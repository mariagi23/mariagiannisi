ADOBE ILLUSTRATOR
For the publication “Understanding Democracy” by the Brandenburg State Centre for Political Education, I had the opportunity to contribute to the visual design and develop a selection of the illustrations. The brochure provides fundamental knowledge about democracy and explains key political terms and concepts in an accessible way.

My illustrations complement the content and translate abstract political topics into an approachable visual language.
